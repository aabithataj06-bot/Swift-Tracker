About SwiftTracker

SwiftTracker is a Salesforce-based CRM for parcel delivery businesses. It manages bookings, dispatch, tracking and delivery confirmation for customers, delivery agents, support staff and admins.

Support team

Resolve delivery issues using Cases and parcel details.

FAQ

How do I get my Parcel ID?

You receive it right after booking, and in the booking confirmation email.

When do I get email updates?

At booking, in transit, out for delivery and on delivery.

Can I change the delivery address?

Contact support before the parcel is Out for Delivery.

Who can see my details?

Only you, the assigned agents and admins. Sensitive fields are restricted.
